## 2026-05-21 - [Jetpack Compose Switch Touch Target & Screen Reader Accessibility]
**Learning:** [A standalone `Switch` component in Compose often has a small touch target. Screen readers may not intuitively associate neighboring text labels with the switch if it acts independently. By applying `Modifier.toggleable` with `Role.Switch` to a larger container (like a `Row`) and setting the inner `Switch`'s `onCheckedChange` to `null`, the entire row becomes the touch target and screen readers announce the element cohesively.]
**Action:** [Apply `Modifier.toggleable()` (with `role = Role.Switch`) to the parent layout (e.g., `Row`) of a `Switch` and its text label, and set the internal `Switch`'s `onCheckedChange` to `null` to improve touch targets and screen reader semantics.]
